The offer

Free includes five protections a day. Lifetime is €4.99, paid once, for unlimited protections. There is no subscription and no renewal charge. Vipra updates through Chrome, the way other extensions do. Those updates are not a second fee.

What you are getting

Vipra highlights types it knows how to see and can swap them for tokens before you send. It will miss things. A clear field means none of those types remain, not that the text is safe, public, or allowed at work.
