What Vipra detects

Only the types it actually looks for.

  • Name[PERSON_1] A person’s name.
  • Email[EMAIL_1] An email address.
  • Phone[PHONE_1] A phone number.
  • IBAN[IBAN_1] An international bank account number.
  • Card[CREDIT_CARD_1] A payment card number.
  • US Social Security number[NATIONAL_ID_1] A US Social Security number.
  • Spanish DNI[DNI_1] A checksum-validated Spanish DNI, replaced on your device.
  • Spanish NIE[NIE_1] A checksum-validated Spanish NIE, replaced on your device.
  • IP address[IP_ADDRESS_1] An IPv4 address.
  • API key[API_KEY_1] Common API key shapes.
  • Token[JWT_1] A JSON Web Token.
  • Secret link[URL_SECRET_1] A link carrying a password or token.

Nicknames and vague hints can slip through. If you are unsure, take them out yourself.

FAQ

Short answers.

Does what I type leave my device?

Only one thing ever goes online: your license key, which Vipra sends to Lemon Squeezy when you activate Lifetime and about once a week after that to confirm it. Never what you type.

Where does it work?

Any text field. AI chats such as ChatGPT are the main case. Email and forms work too. On a few sites the highlights may not line up. If that happens, go by the list in the review panel.

What does it replace?

Names, emails, phones, cards, IBANs, US Social Security numbers and Spanish DNI/NIE, plus a few secret shapes (IPs, API keys, tokens, secret links). Each becomes a token like [EMAIL_1]. A DNI is replaced on your device as [DNI_1], and a NIE as [NIE_1].

What counts as a protection?

Each click on Protect counts as one, and the free count resets every day.

What does free include?

Five protections a day. Lifetime is €4.99 once: unlimited protections and all future updates, on up to 3 devices.

Do I need an account?

No. When you buy Lifetime, you get a license key by email. Paste it into Vipra to unlock. On a new computer, install Vipra and paste the same key.

I got a new laptop. How do I restore Lifetime?

Install Vipra and paste your license key. You can find it in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email, in Lemon Squeezy’s My Orders page (https://app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders), or by writing to hello@vipra.app. One key works on up to 3 devices.
