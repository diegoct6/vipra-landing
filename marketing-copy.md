**Detailed description**

Before you send that to AI.

Vipra finds personal and sensitive information in your prompts and lets you protect it before you send it. A count appears beside the field. Open Review to see what it found, then Protect. Names, emails, and phone numbers become tokens like [PERSON_1], [EMAIL_1], and [PHONE_1].

Local processing. The check runs in your browser. Your prompt is not uploaded to Vipra. No account. No subscription.

Free includes 5 protections every day. Lifetime is €4.99 once for unlimited protections.

It looks for:

- Name
- Email
- Phone
- IBAN
- Card
- National ID (US Social Security number shape)
- IP address (IPv4)
- API key
- Token (JSON Web Token)
- Secret link

ChatGPT’s message field is the one it is built around. It can also review other editable fields, including Gmail. It will not catch every sensitive story. If you are unsure, take it out yourself.

## Short-form

About 25 seconds. Face or screen recording of a ChatGPT box. No fake comments, no “you won’t believe”, no countdown.

1. Say the hook out loud: “Before you send that to AI.”
2. Show a message that still contains a name, an email, and a phone number. Use fake examples (a name you have permission to use, an @example.com address).
3. Point at the review: Name, Email, Phone, each with a token.
4. Tap Protect. The field now shows [PERSON_1], [EMAIL_1], [PHONE_1].
5. Close on one line: “Local. No subscription. Five free a day, or €4.99 once.”

Do not show a real client, a real inbox, or a live card number. A card is something the extension can detect. It is not the story.
