Activate Lifetime

Activation happens in Chrome, not on the website. The full page is Activate Vipra Lifetime.

  1. Open Chrome and click the Vipra puzzle/extension icon (pin it if needed).
  2. In the popup, find “Already purchased?” and paste the license key.
  3. Click Activate. Unlimited protections unlock on this device (up to 3 devices).

Your license key is in the Lemon Squeezy receipt email, and on Lemon Squeezy’s My Orders page.

If it is not there, email us. Include the email used for the purchase, your full name, and what happened.
