Privacy

Privacy

Nothing you type is ever uploaded. Effective 5 October 2026 (Europe/Madrid).

On your device

Detection and replacement happen on your device. Vipra reads the text field you are editing in Chrome, spots personal details, and can swap them for tokens. That includes US Social Security numbers and Spanish DNI/NIE. A checksum-validated DNI is replaced on your device as [DNI_1], and a NIE as [NIE_1]. That text never leaves the computer.

The license key

The only network request is your license key, which Vipra sends to Lemon Squeezy (the merchant of record) when you activate Lifetime and in roughly weekly rechecks after that, to confirm the key. The check does not include what you type.

Support

If you email support, your email and name are used only to verify your purchase.

Analytics and cookies

No analytics. No tracking cookies.
