Privacy

Privacy

Nothing you type leaves your device.

On your device

Vipra reads the text field you are editing so it can spot personal details and replace them with tokens. That check runs in Chrome. The text is not uploaded.

Lifetime activation

The license key is checked only when you activate Lifetime, through Lemon Squeezy. That check sends the key, not what you typed.

No account

You can install Vipra and use the free protections without an account. Free is five protections a day. Lifetime is €4.99, paid once.
